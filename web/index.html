Method & sources

Backblaze reports production hard drive models that meet its quarterly inclusion criteria. A drive-day counts one drive observed for one day. Each annualized failure rate equals failures × 365 ÷ drive-days × 100, rounded to two decimal places. A drive count is a snapshot at quarter end.

A missing row means the model was absent from that quarter’s published table. Zero observed failures are possible. These fleet results describe Backblaze’s workload and do not predict when a particular disk will fail.
